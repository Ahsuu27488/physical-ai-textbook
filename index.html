Sixteen weeks, from sensor to speech

Follow it as a semester or dip in by topic. Each block opens with clear objectives and ends with something that runs.

Open week 1
  1. Weeks 1–2

    Start here + foundations

    Set up your workstation; Python, linear algebra, probability and the Linux toolbelt.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Robot fundamentals

    Frames, kinematics, dynamics and PID control on a simulated arm.

  3. Weeks 5–6

    ROS 2

    Nodes, topics, actions, TF2 and URDF: a whole robot that talks to itself.

  4. Week 7

    Simulation

    Gazebo worlds, simulated sensors, Isaac Sim, and the sim-to-real gap.

  5. Weeks 8–9

    Perception

    Images, point clouds, detection, Kalman filters and SLAM.

  6. Week 10

    Motion

    Path planning, Nav2, MoveIt 2 and grasping.

  7. Weeks 11–12

    Robot learning

    Imitation learning, reinforcement learning and training in simulation.

  8. Week 13

    Humanoids

    Balance, walking, whole-body control and dexterous hands.

  9. Week 14

    Foundation models

    LLM planners, vision-language grounding and VLA models.

  10. Weeks 15–16

    Capstone & career

    Ship a voice-commanded robot assistant and turn it into a portfolio.

Example

When should I use a service instead of a topic?

Rivet

Use a service for a request that needs one reply, like “reset the arm”. Use a topic for a stream, like camera frames or joint states.
